Fire-Fork Kitchen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Fire-Fork Kitchen is a cooking dashboard designed to help users follow recipes, record temperatures, track cooking progress, and create cooking reports.

1. Cooking Data

Cooking sessions, checked steps, temperatures, and notes may be stored locally in your browser. These records are not automatically shared with other visitors or devices.

2. Google Account and Cookbook Access

Fire-Fork Kitchen is being developed to support optional Google account authorization for accessing recipes stored in Google Sheets and Google Docs.

When this feature becomes available, users will be asked to authorize access. Recipe information will be used to display and follow recipes inside the application. The planned integration is read-only and will not modify or delete Google Drive files.

Google authorization is not required to use the basic cooking dashboard.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Cooking sessions are not intentionally shared with other website visitors. Users may choose to export and share their own reports.

4. Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with website visits according to its own privacy policy.

5. PDF Downloads

When a cooking report is exported, the resulting PDF is downloaded through your browser. The download location depends on your device and browser settings.

6. Data Control

You can remove locally stored cooking data by clearing this website's browser storage. If Google authorization is enabled, you can revoke access through your Google Account's third-party connection settings.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as features change, including the introduction of Google cookbook access.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to thelenockers@gmail.com .
