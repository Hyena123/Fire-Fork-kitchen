Fire-Fork Kitchen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Fire-Fork Kitchen is a cooking dashboard designed to help users follow recipes, record temperatures, track cooking progress, and create cooking reports.

1. Cooking Data

During an unfinished cook, this browser stores a recipe copy, checked steps, temperature readings, draft entries and notes for your Google account. Reopen in the same browser and sign in with that account to resume. Both finish options remove the cook and its recipe copy from the dashboard.

2. Google Account and Cookbook Access

Google sign-in identifies your account. The app requests read-only Sheets and Docs access, reads the configured family index and loads the recipe you select. Google controls file access. Access tokens stay in memory. An open cook remains usable when Google access expires. Sign out to lock the dashboard on a shared device.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Ask ChatGPT copies your cook notes, readings and recipe text to your clipboard. You decide whether to paste and send them. Downloads and clipboard copies you create are under your control.

4. Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with website visits according to its own privacy policy.

5. PDF Downloads

Save to PDF opens the print dialog. The report includes the recipe name, times, progress count, temperatures and notes. After saving, confirm to remove the cook and its recipe copy. If you cancel printing, keep the cook open.

6. Data Control

You can remove locally stored cooking data by clearing this website's browser storage. If Google authorization is enabled, you can revoke access through your Google Account's third-party connection settings.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as features change, including changes to cookbook access.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to thelenockers@gmail.com .
